# Privacy & Regulatory Readiness

## Readiness Controls
- Identify potentially sensitive fields.
- Minimize unnecessary data exposure.
- Apply classification-based access.
- Preserve processing lineage.
- Maintain auditable processing decisions.
- Define retention and deletion controls.
- Escalate uncertain legal interpretations to qualified human reviewers.

## Olist Dataset Boundary

The Olist dataset is documented as anonymized commercial data in Area 06. This project does not assume that anonymization alone eliminates every privacy or compliance obligation.

## Regulatory Boundary

The project demonstrates readiness-oriented controls but does not claim formal GDPR, LGPD, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other certification.

## Human Review

Legal and regulatory interpretations require qualified human review.
