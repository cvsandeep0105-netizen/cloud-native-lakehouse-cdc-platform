# Governance Operational Drill

## Controlled Sequence

1. Governance issue detected
2. Dataset identified
3. Data owner identified
4. Classification reviewed
5. Lineage reviewed
6. Access boundary reviewed
7. DQ and reconciliation evidence reviewed
8. Retention requirement reviewed
9. Compliance impact escalated
10. Human governance decision recorded
11. Corrective action authorized
12. Evidence retained and issue closed

AI may summarize evidence but cannot authorize governance decisions.
