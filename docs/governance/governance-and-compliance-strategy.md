# Governance & Compliance Strategy

## Purpose

Define governance and compliance-readiness controls for the Cloud-Native Lakehouse, CDC & AI-Ready Data Platform.

## Governance Principles
- Explicit data ownership.
- Defined stewardship.
- Purpose-based access.
- Data quality accountability.
- Metadata and lineage traceability.
- Controlled retention and deletion.
- Auditable processing.
- Reproducible evidence.
- Human accountability for governance decisions.

## Governance Domains
- Data ownership
- Data stewardship
- Access governance
- Data quality
- Metadata
- Lineage
- Retention
- Privacy
- Auditability
- Compliance evidence

## Compliance Boundary

This project demonstrates compliance readiness controls. It does not claim formal regulatory certification or legal compliance certification.
